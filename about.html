"Recognizing Talent, Inspiring the World"

The Guinex Record Council is dedicated to identifying, documenting, and celebrating remarkable achievements — from a toddler's first big milestone to a lifetime of mastery in a craft. Every record on this roll is reviewed and presented in keeping with the Council's mission: to give talent, at every age and in every field, a record worth keeping.

Based in Thuraiyur, Trichy District, Tamil Nadu, the Council works with individuals, families, schools, and academies across the region to bring their achievements to a wider audience, one certificate at a time.
